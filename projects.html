Skill verification for blue-collar workers. Workers prove skills with a video test and get a shareable Skill Passport. Employers post jobs and find verified tradespeople by trade, city, and wage.

Two-sided app: worker OTP login (Hindi/English), profile, video upload, realtime status, public QR passport.

Employer side: login, job posts, worker search with filters, tracked actions (view, call, WhatsApp, hire).

Admin panel: review queue, signed video URLs, rubric scoring, passport slug on approval.

Supabase RLS on all tables, Realtime + polling for status, i18n, ~18 routes, 7 tables, 3 user roles.
